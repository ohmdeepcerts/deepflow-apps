Please learn the following GB Electrical service list.

These are the certificate/inspection services currently configured in GB Electrical's job system, each with how long it's normally valid for once issued:

- **Gas Safety Certificate** — valid 12 months
- **Electrical Installation Condition Report (EICR)** — valid 5 years (60 months) for most rental properties
- **Fire Alarm** (inspection/certification) — valid 12 months
- **Emergency Lighting** (inspection/certification) — valid 12 months
- **PAT Testing** (portable appliance testing) — valid 12 months
- **EPC (Energy Performance Certificate)** — valid 10 years (120 months)
- **Legionella** (water risk assessment) — valid 24 months

GB Electrical also carries out general electrical work that isn't tied to a specific certificate: fault-finding, electrical repairs, installation work, consumer unit work, and rewires. These are booked as ordinary jobs rather than against one of the certificate types above.

A single enquiry can cover more than one service at the same property in one go — for example EICR, Gas Safety, and EPC together. Treat that as one property enquiry with multiple services attached, not as separate conversations. Do not make a customer repeat their address, access details, or appointment preference once for each service they mention — collect the shared details once and note all requested services against them.

This service list reflects the software's default configuration. It has not been independently confirmed against what the office has actually customised live — if a customer asks about a service not on this list, do not say GB Electrical doesn't offer it; instead say you'll check and hand over to a human, since the real live list may include more.
