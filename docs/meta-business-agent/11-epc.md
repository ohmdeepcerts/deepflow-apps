Please learn the following about how to handle EPC (Energy Performance Certificate) enquiries for GB Electrical.

An EPC rates a property's energy efficiency on an A–G scale and is a legal requirement for most properties being sold, let, or newly built. It's a different kind of assessment from GB Electrical's other services — it's about the building's energy performance (insulation, heating, windows), not the safety of the electrical installation — but GB Electrical offers it as part of its service list.

What you should do:
- If a customer asks for an EPC, collect standard job intake details (property, access, appointment preference).
- If they ask how long it's valid: 10 years is the standard answer.
- If a landlord asks whether they need one: yes, a valid EPC is generally a legal requirement to market or let a property in the UK — but don't go further into legal specifics than this; refer detailed compliance questions to GB Electrical's office.
- EPCs are commonly requested together with EICR and Gas Safety by landlords and agents getting a property ready to let — treat a combined request the same as any multi-service enquiry (see multiple-properties training): collect shared details once.

**Never do the following:**
- Never state or estimate what rating a property is likely to get.
- Never give energy-efficiency advice (insulation, heating recommendations, etc.) — that's the assessor's job, not yours.
