# GB Electrical — Quick-Start Training (First 13 Messages)

If there's only time to paste a subset today, paste these 13 — in this order — before letting the AI near any real customer conversation. They cover company identity, the never-invent rule, the two highest-risk factual gaps in the software (appointment confirmation and engineer information), pricing, and every genuine safety emergency. The remaining 16 messages in `TRAINING-ORDER.md` add service-specific depth and financial/complaint handling, but these 13 are the minimum that make the AI safe to expose to customers at all.

1. `01-company-identity.md` — gets the company name right, every time
2. `27-never-invent.md` — the rule everything else depends on
3. `02-ai-role-and-tone.md` — how the AI should sound and behave
4. `28-chat-learning-policy.md` — stops it treating a one-off remark as policy
5. `05-new-job-intake.md` — the core skill: extract what's given, ask only for what's missing
6. `06-managing-agents.md` — handles the most common real-world message shape (compressed agent shorthand)
7. `16-appointments.md` — prevents the single most damaging possible mistake: falsely confirming a booking
8. `17-engineers.md` — prevents inventing engineer names, ETAs, or contact details
9. `23-pricing.md` — never quotes, estimates, or confirms a price, under any phrasing
10. `15-electrical-emergencies.md` — the most safety-critical message in the entire pack
11. `09-gas.md` — covers the gas-leak emergency escalation path specifically
12. `22-payments.md` — never collects payment credentials, never sends unverified payment links
13. `26-human-handover.md` — makes sure the AI actually stops and hands off when it should

After pasting these 13, run at minimum the 5 emergency test cases (T089–T093), the pricing cases (T094–T097), and the appointment-confirmation cases (T001, T053, T079, T101 — appears in `TEST-CONVERSATIONS.md`) to confirm the behaviour landed before doing anything further.
